# Retail Sales \& Customer Analytics Dashboard



### Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales performance, profit trends, customer segments, product performance, and regional insights.



### Tools Used

* SQL Server
* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Visualization

### 

### Objectives

* Analyze sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Detect loss-making products
* Understand customer and regional trends
* Build executive-level business dashboards



### Dashboard Pages

1\. Executive Sales Overview

2\. Product Performance

3\. Customer \& Region Insights



### Key Insights

* Identified top revenue-generating categories and products
* Analyzed profit margin and loss-making products
* Compared sales performance across customer segments
* Visualized regional sales and profitability trends



### Business Impact

The dashboard helps business teams monitor revenue, optimize product strategy, identify low-performing areas, and support data-driven decision-making.

* SQL querying
* Business intelligence
* Power BI dashboarding
* DAX measures
* KPI reporting
